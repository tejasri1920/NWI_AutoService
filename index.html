Towing & Recovery

The Right Truck for Every Tow

Every vehicle moves differently. We match the equipment to the job, whether that's a low sports car, an all-wheel-drive SUV, or a pickup buried in a ditch off Cline Ave.

  • Flatbed Towing

    The whole vehicle rides on the bed with no wheels on the pavement. It's the safest way to move AWD and 4x4 vehicles, low-clearance cars, and anything that won't roll.

    Best for AWD & 4x4 · luxury & lowered cars · non-running vehicles

  • Wheel-Lift Towing

    Fast, compact, and ideal for tight spots like parking garages, alleys, and crowded lots where a flatbed can't maneuver.

    Best for Front- or rear-wheel-drive cars · tight access · short local tows

  • Winch-Out & Recovery

    Slid into a ditch, stuck in snow or mud, or off the pavement after a spin-out? We winch you back onto solid ground carefully, and tow you in only if it's needed.

    Best for Ditches & embankments · snow & mud · off-road recovery

  • Accident and collision tows to body shops or storage
